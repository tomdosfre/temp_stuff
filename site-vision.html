1 · The model

Every visitor arrives mid-story, carrying a feeling they can't phrase yet: "my work is changing under me." The site's first move is recognition. The claim states the worldview; the question makes it personal; two doors let people name themselves in one sentence. Everything else is proof. Every path ends at the same single ask, because a list signup is the only conversion that compounds while you walk.
